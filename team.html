How to be part of it

Six ways to put your hand up

Every alumnus can do at least one. Every programme is optional, and you choose how much time to give.

01Mentor

An hour a month for a student or a younger alumnus.

02Speaker

A talk at school, or a seat on a career panel.

03Employer

An internship, an opening or a referral.

04Guest or guide

A vodcast story, or a guide for those moving abroad.

05Organiser

Run an event in the inter-school competition.

06Supporter

Sponsor a prize, a stall or a CSR project.
