About MAA

By the alumni, for the alumni.

The Mahatma Alumni Association is an independent, alumni-run body that keeps 20,000+ Mahatmites connected to each other and to the school that raised them.

Our vision

A Mahatma family of 20,000+ alumni, connected for life, and growing the school that raised them.

Our mission

To connect every alumnus with their batch, their teachers and each other; to contribute time, skills and support to the school and the community; and to celebrate where we began.

Why it matters

A school's greatest work walks out of its gates every year.

Homecoming is the day we walk back in, together, and decide what we build with 20,000 alumni.

How we work

Independent. Open. Alumni-run.

The principles behind everything in this website.

Independent

MAA is an alumni-run body. No money is collected from the school.

Recognised

The school gave a No Objection Certificate and signed an MOU. It recognises MAA as its alumni association and monitors its activities.

Open books

Accounts are audited by an independent auditor. The school receives MAA's accounts, data and reports.

Child-safe

Every programme that involves students follows the school's child-safety rules.

Our story

How MAA began

MAA was started by the 2008 batch, which still spearheads it. Today it is run by a Core Council, an Organising Council, committees and a representative from every batch.
