Questions

The FAQ

When is Homecoming?

Sunday, 27 December 2026, in Madurai. The School Visit Days are on Friday 25 and Saturday 26 December.

How do I register, and what does it cost?

Register at www.mahatmaschools.com. The ticket is ₹1,299 + GST.

What happens on the day?

The programme, a panel, a networking session, lunch for all attendees and the launch of the MAA app.

What are the School Visit Days?

Free visits to the school from 10 am to 4 pm, with lunch provided, for alumni and family. Sign up through the visit form (link coming soon).

Can I bring my family?

Yes, to the School Visit Days. Please mention the number in the visit form.

Do I have to join any programme?

No. Every programme is optional. You choose how much time to give.

Who do I contact?

Write to alumni@mahatmaschools.com.
