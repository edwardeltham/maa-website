Why we exist

Vision and mission

A school's greatest work walks out of its gates every year. We are how it walks back in, together.

Our vision

A Mahatma family of 20,000+ alumni, connected for life, and growing the school that raised them.

Our mission

To connect every alumnus with their batch, their teachers and each other; to contribute time, skills and support to the school and the community; and to celebrate where we began.

From one day to a year-round network

The first six months

A proposed plan. Light programmes start first, heavier ones follow once the team is steady.

  1. 25–26 Dec

    School Visit Days

    Alumni offer topics to teach and talk about.

  2. 27 Dec

    Homecoming

    All sign-ups open, the MAA app launches, pledges are collected.

  3. January

    Matching begins

    Mentor matching starts, vodcast guests are chosen, an openings board is set up.

  4. Feb–Mar

    First visits and first jobs

    Resource-person visits are scheduled, first internships and referrals happen, CSR needs are shortlisted.

  5. Apr–Jun

    Eight schools, one rivalry

    The inter-school competition is planned and run; the first CSR project is reported.

A proposed order of work, not a promise. Dates depend on school calendars and volunteers.

How to be part of it

Six ways to put your hand up

Every alumnus can do at least one. Every programme is optional, and you choose how much time to give.

01Mentor

An hour a month for a student or a younger alumnus.

02Speaker

A talk at school, or a seat on a career panel.

03Employer

An internship, an opening or a referral.

04Guest or guide

A vodcast story, or a guide for those moving abroad.

05Organiser

Run an event in the inter-school competition.

06Supporter

Sponsor a prize, a stall or a CSR project.
