Programmes

Nine programmes, four purposes.

Every one is optional. Pick what suits your life, and give as much or as little time as you choose.

27 DecA · Guide

Mentorship

Guidance from someone who has walked the road, one conversation at a time.

How it works

  • Mentors and mentees sign up with their field and goals
  • MAA matches them by field, city and interest
  • Monthly one-to-one talks for six months (proposed)

Alumni give

  • About one hour a month
  • Honest advice on studies, choices and first jobs
  • An introduction to their network

Everyone gains

  • Mentees find direction early
  • Mentors find purpose and perspective
  • Batches meet across years

Starts 27 Dec: mentor and mentee sign-ups.

27 DecA · Guide

Careers

Know what the work is really like, from people who do it.

How it works

  • Alumni list their profession and field
  • Short talks, panels and Q&A in school and online
  • A directory by field, so students know whom to ask

Alumni give

  • A talk, a panel seat or a 15-minute call
  • An honest account of the path, detours included
  • Pointers on skills and courses to pick up

Everyone gains

  • Students meet careers they have never seen
  • Alumni become role models within reach
  • Better choices before the exam results

Starts 27 Dec: alumni add their profession and field at sign-up.

27 DecA · Guide

Moving abroad

Someone who has already made the move, a message away.

How it works

  • Alumni abroad register their country and city
  • One peer group per country for questions
  • Sessions on applications, moving and settling in

Alumni give

  • Their lessons from the first year
  • Pointers on applications and first weeks
  • A familiar face on arrival, where possible

Everyone gains

  • Fewer costly mistakes for those moving
  • A welcome waiting in the new city
  • Alumni abroad stay close to home

Starts 27 Dec: alumni abroad register where they live. Peer guidance only, not legal or immigration advice.

27 DecB · Open doors

Internships

A first real experience, through people the school already trusts.

How it works

  • Alumni businesses and teams list short internships
  • Students and young alumni apply through MAA
  • A mentor or contact checks in during the stint

Alumni give

  • A place, or a small project, for a few weeks
  • A supervisor who explains the work
  • Feedback and a reference at the end

Everyone gains

  • Early experience and a first reference
  • Businesses meet motivated young talent
  • The network proves itself in practice

Starts 27 Dec: alumni employers register the openings they can offer.

After 27 DecB · Open doors

Jobs

A warm introduction travels further than a résumé.

How it works

  • Alumni post openings in their teams and companies
  • Job seekers share a short profile with MAA
  • Alumni refer, introduce and advise

Alumni give

  • An opening posted, or a referral made
  • A quick résumé review
  • A mock interview for a batchmate

Everyone gains

  • Job seekers get introductions, not cold applications
  • Employers meet candidates they can trust
  • Trust in the network keeps growing

Starts after 27 Dec: an openings board and a profile list on the MAA platform.

25–26 DecC · Give back

Alumni as resource persons

Our classrooms deserve our best people, and they are our alumni.

How it works

  • Alumni pick a topic they know well
  • The school schedules visits through the year
  • Talks, workshops and demonstrations for students

Alumni give

  • A morning or an hour on campus
  • Expertise in their field, explained simply
  • A story from their own school days

Everyone gains

  • Students hear from working professionals
  • Teachers get fresh support in class
  • Alumni return to where it began

Starts 25 and 26 Dec: School Visit Days, where alumni can offer a topic.

27 DecC · Give back

CSR and community

Giving back together, with a plan and open accounts.

How it works

  • The school and alumni name the needs together
  • Projects are chosen, costed and tracked openly
  • Funds and volunteers come through MAA

Alumni give

  • Time and skills on a project
  • Funds, large or small
  • Their company's CSR support, where possible

Everyone gains

  • Students and the community see real results
  • Alumni employers find a meaningful local project
  • Open accounts build lasting trust

Starts 27 Dec: pledges of time, skills and funds, and a shortlist of needs from the school.

Open callD · Connect and celebrate

Alumni Talks vodcast

Our stories, told by us, in our own voices.

How it works

  • A regular episode with one alumni guest, interviewed by students
  • School memories, a turning point and one lesson
  • Full episodes on the school YouTube channel, plus short clips

Alumni give

  • An hour of conversation as a guest
  • Nominations of batchmates with a story
  • Skills in hosting, editing or design

Everyone gains

  • Role models students can hear and see
  • Batches rediscover one another
  • A growing archive of the school's story

Open call: guest nominations and a volunteer production team.

After 27 DecD · Connect and celebrate

MAA inter-school competition

Eight schools, one friendly rivalry, organised by alumni.

How it works

  • Alumni design events: for example quiz, debate, sports, arts
  • Each of the 8 schools sends its teams
  • One finals day, alumni as organisers and judges

Alumni give

  • Time to organise, judge and coach
  • Prizes and sponsorship
  • Venues, equipment and refreshments

Everyone gains

  • Students get a stage and learn teamwork
  • Eight schools feel like one family
  • Alumni become organisers they remember

Starts after 27 Dec: the council confirms the format and calendar; alumni volunteer to run events.

Every programme is a proposal until the council confirms it. Dates are indicative.

What's in it for the alumni

You get far more than a reunion

Belong

  • Friends and teachers found again
  • A school that still counts you as its own
  • Pride in where you began

Grow

  • Mentors in your field
  • Career guidance from people who have done it
  • Peers abroad who know the way

Earn

  • Warm introductions to jobs
  • Trusted candidates and interns for your own team
  • Business among batchmates

Give

  • A classroom talk, in the school you remember
  • A project you can see and follow
  • A story told on the vodcast

What it costs: a registration, and as much or as little time as you choose.

What's in it for the school

A school with 20,000 people behind it

Students

  • Role models they can meet
  • Career talks and internships
  • A stage in the inter-school competition

Teachers

  • Guest speakers for their classes
  • Volunteers for events
  • Recognition from former students

Resources

  • Skills and time on tap
  • Funds for projects, tracked openly
  • CSR support from alumni companies

Standing

  • Alumni who stay connected and proud
  • Stories of where graduates went
  • Eight schools working as one family

What the school gives: a point of contact, permission for visits, and child-safety rules that every programme follows.

The first six months

From one day to a year-round network

  1. 25–26 Dec

    School Visit Days

    Alumni offer topics to teach and talk about.

  2. 27 Dec

    Homecoming

    All sign-ups open, the MAA app launches, pledges are collected.

  3. January

    Matching begins

    Mentor matching starts, vodcast guests are chosen, an openings board is set up.

  4. Feb–Mar

    First visits and first jobs

    Resource-person visits are scheduled, first internships and referrals happen, CSR needs are shortlisted.

  5. Apr–Jun

    Eight schools, one rivalry

    The inter-school competition is planned and run; the first CSR project is reported.

A proposed order of work, not a promise. Dates depend on school calendars and volunteers.
